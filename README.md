# joyscroller

joyscroller keeps a permanent copy of every video you've liked, so a like never disappears when the platform hides it, deletes it, or buries it in an endless feed. It runs on your own computer and signs in with the browser you already use: it reads your login cookies straight from your Chromium-family browser (Brave, Chrome, Chromium, Vivaldi or Edge), so there's no account to connect. Every five minutes it saves new likes to a local database, and in the background it downloads each video and its thumbnail at the best quality available. YouTube and TikTok work today, and Instagram is planned. For TikTok it briefly runs your browser headless in the background, with a separate throwaway profile, because TikTok only answers requests made from inside its own web page. A web page on localhost for browsing your archive is on the way.

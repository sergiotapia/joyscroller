# joyscroller

joyscroller keeps a permanent copy of every video you've liked, so a like never disappears when the platform hides it, deletes it, or buries it in an endless feed. It runs on your own computer and signs in with the browser you already use: it reads your login cookies straight from your Chromium-family browser (Brave, Chrome, Chromium, Vivaldi or Edge), so there's no account to connect. Every five minutes it saves new likes to a local database. YouTube works today, and Instagram and TikTok are planned. A web page on localhost for browsing your archive is on the way.
